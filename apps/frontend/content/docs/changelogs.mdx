## Latest Release: 1.13.0 - 2026-11-11

<Card

Made for the digital instructions the EU Machinery Regulation allows from 20 January 2027.</li>

        <li><strong>Page Templates & Live Demos:</strong> Ready-made designs for restaurant menus, café & bar, real estate, brochures, event programs, price lists, machines, devices, household appliances and tools, with three page styles, font pairings, light/dark mode and your own colors. Every template has a live demo page.</li>

        <li><strong>Phone, SMS & WhatsApp QR Codes:</strong> Three new content types — call a number, send a prefilled SMS or open a WhatsApp chat — including CSV bulk import.</li>
        <li><strong>Free QR Code Scanner:</strong> A new <a href="/tools/qr-code-scanner">tool page</a> reads QR codes from images, the clipboard or the camera, entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.</li>

        <li><strong>Extra Custom Domains:</strong> Pro accounts can add more custom domains as an add-on: €3.99/month per domain, or €2.99/month billed annually.</li>
        <li><strong>Self-Verifying Domains:</strong> Pending custom domains are checked automatically every 15 minutes for 7 days, and the dashboard explains when a CAA record blocks the certificate.</li>

Create a QR code for the page you're on in one click: the extension fills in the page's link, gives you the full generator, and saves your QR codes to your account. It uses your existing sign-in from qrcodly.de, so there is nothing new to set up.</li>
        <li><strong>Figma Plugin (coming soon):</strong> Browse, search and filter your QR codes in Figma, create URL and text codes from a template and place them on the canvas. It appears in Settings → Integrations and goes live once Figma approves it.</li>

      </ul>

        <li><strong>Detailed Analytics in Pro:</strong> Every plan keeps the headline figures — totals, visitors, the last 7 days, top country and top device. Activity over time and breakdowns by device, browser, operating system and country are part of Pro.</li>
      </ul>

      <h4>🎨 UI & UX Improvements</h4>
      <ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
        <li><strong>Refreshed Website:</strong> Home, product and solution pages in one new look; the features page now lives on the home page.</li>
        <li><strong>New Navigation:</strong> Products, Solutions and Resources menus in the header.</li>
        <li><strong>Shorter Download Menu:</strong> The QR code submenu is skipped when the image is the only thing to download.</li>
        <li><strong>Calmer Page Editor on Phones:</strong> Steps open and close without the page jumping, and after saving, the generator stays in view.</li>
        <li><strong>Layout in Every Language:</strong> Long labels no longer spill out of the generator's type buttons and the page editor; product and marketing pages are tuned for phones, tablets and laptops in all 9 languages.</li>
